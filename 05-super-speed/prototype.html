We've fixed how missed pings affect your responders

A 4.2 change meant some responders were ranked lower for pings they never received. Here's what we've changed.

What went wrong4.2 shortened the ping wait from 90 to 60 seconds, and some pings weren't reaching responders' phones. Both were counted as if the responder had said no, so some responders were offered fewer and fewer callouts.
What's changedThe ping wait is back to 90 seconds. Dispatch now confirms whether each ping reached the phone. A ping that never arrived doesn't count against your responder, and one that arrived but wasn't answered counts less than a turn-down.
The resetResponders ranked lower after 4.2 have been restored to where they stood on 12 August. This is a one-time correction.
