# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, type wrap up, and Claude Code saves the
prompts you wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt — updates
CLAUDE.md, and saves your work to GitHub. By Module 6 this file is a
prompt library built from your own questions.

---

### 1.
Based solely on the interviews, help me organize the feedback into categories and a suggested prioritization?

### 2.
What data could we look at to quantify this feedback?

### 3.
Was there anything else important that was mentioned during the interviews even if it was a small n, that I should know about?

### 4.
Use the rook-database connector to read the support_tickets table. Same treatment as before: group them, tell me how many are in each group, and quote one line from each.

### 5.
Help me find if there is any correlation between the tickets and interview feedback. If there is, summarize those in a table with counts.

### 6.
Let's dig into the matching wording on calls, I want to understand the potential impact of this on the ticket counts so I don't over index for the same issue being reported by the same person.

### 7.
You've now read both folders (interviews and tickets). Where do they disagree? What's loud in the interviews but rare in the tickets, and what's all over the tickets that nobody brought up in the interviews?

### 8.
Please remind me who are the tickets coming from? The handlers or someone else?

### 9.
Given all the data we now have, what are the next steps you would recommend?

### 10.
Poke holes at the suggested next steps, what am I missing or dropping the ball on?

### 11.
Help me define the success measure and draft a plan for it.
